I am a PhD student in Mathematics at the University of Coimbra. My research is in partial differential equations, and more specifically in free boundary problems: problems where the interface between two regimes is itself an unknown.

A free boundary: the obstacle problem

A membrane pinned at its ends rests on an obstacle. Where it touches (the contact set) it follows the obstacle; elsewhere it is a straight line. The red points, the edges of the contact set, are the free boundary: they are not known in advance but are found as part of the solution. Raise the obstacle to see them move.
